A guide to 14 landing page formats, defining a landing page as a standalone web page built for one goal: getting visitors to take a single action. Types covered include webinar registration, ebook, ecommerce, lead magnet, squeeze, podcast, PPC, thank you, video, link-in-bio, pre-launch, Facebook, lead capture, and click-through pages, with guidance on when to use each.
